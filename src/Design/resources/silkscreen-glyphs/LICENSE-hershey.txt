hershey-roman-simplex.txt — licence
===================================

The glyph coordinates in hershey-roman-simplex.txt are the Roman Simplex glyphs of the Hershey
fonts, converted into this file's own format from the Usenet distribution of the fonts. The Hershey
fonts are in the public domain. That distribution may be used by anyone for any purpose, commercial
or otherwise, provided that the following acknowledgements are distributed with the font data:

  - The Hershey Fonts were originally created by Dr. A. V. Hershey while working at the U. S.
    National Bureau of Standards.
  - The format of the Font data in this distribution was originally created by
        James Hurt
        Cognition, Inc.
        900 Technology Park Drive
        Billerica, MA 01821

and that the font data is not converted into the format distributed by the U.S. National Technical
Information Service (NTIS). hershey-roman-simplex.txt is not in that format.

Only the 39 glyphs a reference designator is written in were taken (A-Z, 0-9, '-', '_', '+').
hershey-variants.txt holds three further glyphs (two 1s and a 3) assembled from the strokes of the
Hershey Roman Simplex glyphs and the Hershey serif Roman face, and is covered by this licence in the
same way. They are used as matching templates for stroked silkscreen text (circuitRF's Create
Schematic from Artwork); nothing is drawn with them.
